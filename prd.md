# PRD #5: Pokemon go victor

## Starters
4 starters, palkia, dialga, arceus, Eternatus.

## Win or lose
you battle a pokemon, if you win, you can eliminate it, or catch it: (regular ball 50/100 percent) (legendary ball 70/100 percent) (master ball 90/100 percent)
Lose: you have to revive your pokemon

## Server decides
whether you can catch the Pokémon, whether you can upgrade your Pokémon, your final power and score.

## Screens
title, arena, results

## Out of scope
more than one trail
moves and type matchups
multiplayer
